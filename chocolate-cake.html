Classic Chocolate Cake 🍫

Rich, moist, and absolutely irresistible!

← Back to Home

🛒 Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups sugar
  • ¾ cup cocoa powder
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1½ tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • ½ cup oil
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • 1 cup boiling water

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease two 9-inch cake pans.
  2. Mix flour, sugar, cocoa, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  3. Add milk, oil, eggs, and vanilla. Beat for 2 minutes.
  4. Stir in boiling water (batter will be thin).
  5. Pour into pans and bake for 30-35 minutes.
  6. Cool for 10 minutes, then remove from pans.
  7. Frost and enjoy!
