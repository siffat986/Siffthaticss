Chocolate Chip Cookies 🍪

Chewy, gooey, and loaded with chocolate!

← Back to Home

🛒 Ingredients

  • 2¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 cups chocolate chips

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. Mix flour, baking soda, and salt.
  3. Beat butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar until creamy.
  4. Add eggs one at a time, then vanilla.
  5. Gradually add flour mixture, then stir in chocolate chips.
  6. Drop tablespoons onto baking sheets.
  7. Bake for 9-11 minutes until golden brown.
  8. Cool on baking sheet for 5 minutes.
